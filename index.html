How I audit an account

Six steps from “sales are down” to a plan with numbers

The same order on every account, so nothing gets missed. Scroll through a sample audit.

  1. Pull the data

    Business reports, the ads console, inventory and account health, for the same two periods.

  2. Find where sales went

    Child ASIN by ASIN: sessions, conversion, Buy Box and price, so the cause is clear.

  3. Check ads term by term

    Every search term gets the same rule: cut, scale or negate, with the money it saves.

  4. Tear down the listing

    Title, bullets, images, A+, size chart and questions, side by side with the leaders.

  5. Turn it into a plan

    Quick wins in two weeks, a lean ads restart, then rank and price. Each step measured.

  6. Report every week

    A WBR the brand can decide from: KPIs, pacing, actions, owners and decisions.
