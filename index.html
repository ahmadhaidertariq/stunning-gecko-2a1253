How a wholesale month runs with me

The same process I use for every store, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Account and budget check

    Your categories, stock budget, documents and the brands you already sell.

    Days 1 to 3

  2. Brand list

    Brands with 200+ sales a month, 3+ FBA sellers and Amazon under half the Buy Box.

    Every week

  3. Outreach and follow-ups

    Applications from your business email, follow-ups every 14 days, every reply logged.

    Every day

  4. Price lists checked

    Every item matched to its ASIN, all fees filled, profit and ROI worked out.

    Same day a list arrives

  5. Buy plan for your OK

    You approve the products and quantities. I request the invoice, you pay the supplier.

    Weekly report
