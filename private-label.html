Common questions

Before we talk.

What access do you need?

User access to your Seller Central and Amazon Ads with the permissions for the work, added by you. I never ask for your password.

How fast will I see results?

Wasted spend usually drops within the first two to four weeks. Ranking and conversion changes from listing work take longer, so I report both separately.

Do you work with new brands?

Yes. For a new brand I start with the audit and launch plan, then build ads around your first keywords.

Do you design images?

I write the image and A+ briefs and can deliver a full image set, like the sample above.

Want a review of your brand?

Send your brand and your main ASINs. I'll reply with what I'd fix first in your listings and ads.
