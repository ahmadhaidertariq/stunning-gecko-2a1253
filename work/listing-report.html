The core differentiator to market

The brand's instruction on how this listing should be written (condensed):

Most hard water removers are acid-heavy and strip the protective coating on modern shower glass. Kestrel is pH-balanced, so it lifts limescale without damaging coated glass, chrome or natural-stone grout. Shoppers should never worry that cleaning will ruin a new shower door. Safe on coated glass vs. strips it clean is the main wedge against every other remover on the page.

Section 3 of 4

Description

The old description repeated the title and gave no directions. The rewrite adds use steps, surface limits and coverage.

After — final copy
Cloudy shower glass usually means hard water, not dirt. Kestrel Clean is a pH-balanced hard water stain remover that dissolves calcium and limescale while staying gentle on coated shower glass, chrome and sealed tile. Spray on a dry surface, wait 3 minutes and wipe with a soft cloth. For heavy buildup, leave it for up to 10 minutes and use a non-scratch sponge. Rinse with water when you are done. Use it on glass shower doors, faucets, shower heads, tile, grout and sinks. Do not use on marble, travertine or unsealed natural stone. One 16 oz bottle covers about 40 full shower cleans. Low odor and bleach-free, so it is comfortable to use in small bathrooms.
