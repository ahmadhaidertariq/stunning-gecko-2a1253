PEBBLE & PINE / AMAZON US · Proving impact06

Sales beyond the ad report

We agree how success is measured before any change goes live.

Total salesNot only ad sales

Track total ordered sales and TACoS, so ads are judged on the whole product.

ContributionAfter advertising

Profit after product cost, fees and ads per ASIN, so growth does not hide losses.

Rules firstAgreed before launch

Test length, minimum clicks and stop-loss limits written down in week 1.
