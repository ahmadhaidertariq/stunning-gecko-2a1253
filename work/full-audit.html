FERRO MOTION / AMAZON US · Executive summary01

The short version

Sales fell 71%. Knee sleeves explain 81% of it, and the cause is visibility, not the product.

Ordered sales-71%$1.12M to $325K, Nov to Sep, year over year
Sessions-74%610K to 159K across all child ASINs
Conversion6.8%Up from 4.9%. Shoppers who land still buy.
Ad spend, last 30 days$9All campaigns paused since about May 2026

What is brokenTraffic

Knee sleeve searches grew 64% while Ferro sleeve sessions fell 80%. Buy Box held, price cuts did not help, and the ad budget went to the weakest campaigns.

What is leaking moneyAged stock and thin prices

Slow sleeve sizes carry about $3.1K a month in aged surcharges, and four SKUs now sell below their Amazon fees.

What still worksA healthy base

Account health is clean (ODR 0.11%). The wrist wraps convert at 18.4%. Last year ads returned $3.05 per $1 spent.

Source: Business Reports, Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item (two 11-month periods); Amazon Ads campaign manager; Manage FBA Inventory.

Key finding

The main problem is visibility, not the product, and visibility on Amazon can be rebuilt.

FERRO MOTION / AMAZON US · Diagnosis05

Why it happened

Conversion went up and Buy Box held. Sessions are the only number that collapsed.

Sleeve sessions-80%323K to 65K, top 6 ASINs
Sleeve conversion5.1%Up from 3.6%
Featured offer96.2%Account level
Sleeve avg price-19%$42.10 to $34.10

Ruled outWhat did not cause it

  • Buy Box loss: 85 to 99% on every major sleeve ASIN in both periods
  • Listing quality: conversion rose on 5 of 6 sleeve ASINs
  • Suppression: all top ASINs active and in stock

Fits the dataWhat did

  • Ad spend cut to zero, removing paid placements
  • Lower sales velocity pulling organic rank down with it
  • Stronger competitors with lower prices and more reviews

Source: Child ASIN reports; Business Reports insights panel; Amazon Ads brand metrics.

Key finding

Shoppers are still searching for knee sleeves. They are just finding competitors first, and that is recoverable.

FERRO MOTION / AMAZON US · Ads audit07

Where the budget went

The worst campaigns got almost twice the budget of the best ones.

Above 45% ACOS (5 campaigns)$8.1K
25 to 45% ACOS (11 campaigns)$9.0K
Below 25% ACOS (8 campaigns)$4.3K

Cut first"SL - Auto Broad 2026"

131% ACOS on $2.6K spend. Loose match and complements carried most of the waste.

Scale first"SL - Exact - knee sleeve women"

14% ACOS, 9.8% CVR, budget capped by 2 pm most days.

Clean upSearch terms

212 search terms spent $5+ with no sale: $2.2K that negatives would have saved.

Source: Sponsored Products campaign report and search term report, 12 months.

Key finding

The fix is not more budget. It is moving the same budget from the bottom bucket to the top one.
