Common questions

Straight answers before we talk.

Do you guarantee brand approvals?

No honest seller can guarantee a brand's decision. I guarantee the work: the right brands, a professional application, follow-ups and every reply logged in your sheet.

What do you need from me?

Your company name, address and phone, a business email to send from, and your reseller certificate and EIN letter as PDF files. Documents are only sent when a brand asks for them.

Do you need my Seller Central password?

No. For outreach I only need access to the business email the applications go from.

Can you work on Walmart too?

Yes. I find brands that sell well on Walmart, where Walmart itself isn't the seller, and apply for them the same way.

Who pays the suppliers?

You do, directly. I prepare the purchase order and request the invoice only after you approve the products and quantities.

Want approvals for your store?

Send your store type, categories and budget. I'll reply with an honest view of what I'd do first, and whether I'm the right fit.
